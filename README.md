## Summary

View and organize Firefox’s "Other Bookmarks" in a clean grid. Use it as your new-tab page or open it from the toolbar.

## Description

bmgrid replaces Firefox’s new-tab page with a clean, minimal grid of the built-in "Other Bookmarks" folder. Browse folders, open bookmarks, and organize them directly from the grid with drag-and-drop.

### Features

- Drag and drop bookmarks and folders to move or reorder them.
- Add, edit, rename, and delete items through right-click menus.
- Follow your Firefox theme automatically, or choose Dark or Light and customize their colors.
- Restore extension-made deletions with `Ctrl+Z` during the browser session.
- Download a log of the latest 100 bookmark-deletion events.

Open the toolbar popup or the extension’s Preferences to choose a theme and customize its colors.

**Browser Theme** is the default. Its colors follow your Firefox theme and update automatically when it changes. These colors cannot be overridden in the extension. Dark and Light each have their own optional color overrides, remembered when you switch themes.

The toolbar icon follows your browser theme’s color. It fills in when the current page is saved in "Other Bookmarks" or any of its nested folders. Pages saved only elsewhere in Firefox leave the icon outlined.

The toolbar popup also offers **Delete Bookmark** for the current page. It immediately deletes all matching copies in "Other Bookmarks" and its nested folders, while keeping copies saved elsewhere. The button appears only when there are matching bookmarks. You can restore the deleted copies individually with `Ctrl+Z` in the grid.

### Green dots and opening bookmarks

A green dot marks a bookmark that is already open in the same browser window. Click the bookmark to switch to its tab without reloading it. If no matching tab is open, it opens in the current tab. Middle-click always opens a new tab.

### Organizing your bookmarks

Right-click a bookmark to edit or delete it. Right-click a folder to add bookmarks and folders to it.

bmgrid works with your actual Firefox bookmarks, so changes also appear in Firefox’s bookmark manager. Only "Other Bookmarks" and its contents are displayed.

Deleting a folder also deletes its contents. Deletion is immediate; `Ctrl+Z` in the grid restores deletions made during the current browser session.
